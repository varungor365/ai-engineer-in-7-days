# ai-engineer-in-7-days - AI Agent Guidelines

## Agent Context
If a user asks you to expand upon the curriculum in this repository, follow these rules:
1. Ensure the tone remains highly accessible and practical. Do not delve into deep mathematical theory unless absolutely necessary.
2. Structure the content with clear `h2` and `h3` tags.
3. Always include a "Homework" section at the end of each day with actionable coding tasks.
